Questions

Things you're about to ask.

We know. We've heard them. Read these before you email Sharon. Sharon will not read your email.

Can I check on it?
No.
I've had a great idea for it since drop-off.
Write it down. Start a new project. Drop that off in fourteen months. The lads are finishing the one you had, not the one you have now.
What if it's only 79% done?
Come back at 80. We have a ruler. We are not a place for starting things. Starting is the one thing you don't need help with.
What counts as "finished"?
Shipped, published, standing, or in a customer's hands. Not "basically done", "just needs polish", "done apart from the roof", or "I could ship it tomorrow if I wanted".
What's in the cut?
A cut. Of the whole thing. If it's a business, a share. If it's a book, royalties. If it's a shed, we keep the good shelf.

Yes. Also lads. The lads are the AI. Please don't ask which is which. Deco gets upset.
